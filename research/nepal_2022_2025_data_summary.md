# Nepal Data Summary (2022-2025) for Phase Space Analysis

## 1. V-Dem (Varieties of Democracy) Trends
*   **Trend Direction:** Negative (Declining Democracy / Increasing Repression).
*   **Key Report:** "Nepal slips in democracy index" (Kathmandu Post, March 8, 2024).
*   **2025 Context:** Reports of a "Gen Z protest crackdown" in late 2025 indicate a sharp spike in state repression.
*   **Estimated Scale for Phase Space:**
    *   **2022:** Moderate Repression (Baseline).
    *   **2023-2024:** Gradual Increase (declining index score).
    *   **Sept 2025:** High Repression (Peak). Evidenced by "mass arrests" and police crackdowns.

## 2. ACLED / Protest Intensity (Public Mobilization)
*   **2022 ("No Not Again"):**
    *   **Type:** Electoral campaign, social media driven, localized street presence.
    *   **Intensity:** Low to Moderate.
    *   **Key Feature:** Anti-incumbency sentiment, non-violent.
*   **2023-2024 (Pro-Monarchy / Coop Scams):**
    *   **Type:** Sporadic street protests, led by figures like Durga Prasai.
    *   **Intensity:** Moderate. Increasing volatility but not regime-threatening.
*   **September 2025 ("Gen Z Uprising"):**
    *   **Type:** Mass uprising, violent riots, regime-threatening.
    *   **Intensity:** **Peak (Very High)**.
    *   **Key Metrics:**
        *   **Arrests:** 580+ people arrested in connection with protests.
        *   **Violence:** Burning of the Hilton Hotel (Kathmandu).
        *   **Outcome:** Resignation of Prime Minister K.P. Sharma Oli.

## 3. Scale of Protests (Quantitative Proxies)
*   **"No Not Again" (2022):** Primarily measured in *voting shifts* (rise of RSP) rather than mass street mobilization.
*   **"Gen Z" (2025):**
    *   **Crowd Size:** Reports imply "thousands" involved in violent clashes.
    *   **Economic Impact:** Rs 20 billion ($150M) in insurance claims (indicates scale of physical destruction).
    *   **Casualties/Arrests:** High number of injuries and 580 confirmed arrests.

## Phase Space Axis Definitions
Based on this data, the axes for the phase space diagram can be defined as:

*   **X-Axis: State Repression (Inverse of Civil Liberties)**
    *   *Range:* 0 (High Liberties) to 1 (High Repression).
    *   *Trajectory:* Moves from ~0.4 (2022) to ~0.8 (Sept 2025).
*   **Y-Axis: Public Mobilization (Protest Intensity)**
    *   *Range:* 0 (Calm) to 1 (Mass Uprising).
    *   *Trajectory:* Moves from ~0.2 (2022) to ~0.9 (Sept 2025).

*Note: Exact V-Dem scores for 2024/2025 are not yet available in public datasets, but the trend is confirmed by qualitative reports of "slipping" democracy and "crackdowns".*
