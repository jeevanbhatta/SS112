# Research Report: Recent Political Protests in Nepal (2023-2025)

## Overview
The search results indicate a significant wave of **"Gen Z Protests"** occurring in Nepal, particularly intensifying in **September 2025**. These protests appear to be a major youth-led uprising driven by dissatisfaction with corruption, political stagnation, and specific government actions like social media crackdowns.

## Key Terminology
*   **"Gen Z"**: The protests are explicitly referred to as "Gen Z protests" in multiple news reports.
*   **"Gen G"**: This specific term was not found in the search results. The "Gen Z" movement is the primary youth-led force identified.

## Major Event: The Gen Z Uprising (September 2025)

### Key Events
*   **September 8-9, 2025**: Violent protests erupted in Kathmandu.
*   **Hilton Hotel Fire**: A major incident involved the burning of the Hilton hotel in Kathmandu on September 9, 2025.
*   **PM Resignation**: Prime Minister **K.P. Sharma Oli** resigned on September 9, 2025, amid the intensifying unrest.
*   **Mass Arrests**: Reports indicate that 580 people were arrested and 278 cases were registered in connection with the protests.
*   **Casualties/Injuries**: There are reports of many injured protesters.

### Causes
1.  **Corruption**: Widespread anger against systemic corruption.
2.  **Social Media Crackdown**: The protests were partly triggered or fueled by a government crackdown on social media.
3.  **Political Stagnation**: A broader demand for a "political reset" and dissatisfaction with the traditional political leadership.
4.  **Economic Issues**: High youth unemployment and economic stagnation (implied by the "political reset" demands and general context).

### Outcomes
*   **Political Change**: Resignation of PM K.P. Sharma Oli.
*   **Interim Government**: References to the dissolution of the House of Representatives and the formation of an interim government with a narrow mandate.
*   **Economic Impact**: Insurance claims related to property damage during the protests crossed **Rs 20 billion** (approx. $150 million USD).
*   **Political Realignment**: The **Rastriya Swatantra Party (RSP)** and **Bibeksheel Sajha** announced a merger to strengthen alternative politics, likely capitalizing on the youth sentiment.
