# Nepal Political History Timeline Data

This document outlines the key events for the timeline visualization, linking protests and movements to the regime changes they precipitated.

## Data Points

| Year | Event Type | Event Name | Description | Source |
|------|------------|------------|-------------|--------|
| 1769 | Regime Change | **Unification of Nepal** | King Prithvi Narayan Shah unifies the kingdom, establishing the Shah Dynasty. | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 1846 | Regime Change | **Rise of Rana Dynasty** | Jung Bahadur Rana seizes power, establishing an autocratic hereditary prime ministership. | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 1951 | Protest/Movement | **1951 Revolution** | A revolution supported by King Tribhuvan and India overthrows the Rana regime. | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 1951 | Regime Change | **End of Rana Rule** | Restoration of the Monarchy and introduction of democratic institutions ("Democratic Interlude"). | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 1996 | Protest/Movement | **Maoist Insurgency Begins** | Launch of the "People's War" by the CPN (Maoist) seeking to overthrow the monarchy. | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 2006 | Protest/Movement | **People's Movement II (Jana Andolan)** | Mass protests and the peace process lead to the Comprehensive Peace Agreement (CPA). | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 2008 | Regime Change | **Abolition of Monarchy** | Nepal is declared a Federal Democratic Republic, ending 240 years of Shah rule. | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 2015 | Regime Change | **New Constitution** | Promulgation of the 2015 Constitution, solidifying the federal democratic structure. | [PolSci Institute](https://polsci.institute/south-asia/nepal-history-monarchy-to-democratic-republic/) |
| 2022 | Protest/Movement | **Gen Z / "No Not Again" Protests** | Decentralized youth-led protests targeting corruption and traditional political parties. | [ACLED](https://acleddata.com) |
